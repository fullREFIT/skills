# Example 2: Monthly Financial Close SOP

## Context

- **Process:** Monthly financial close (bank reconciliation through board package)
- **Source:** 2-page meeting notes
- **Company type:** Mid-market company with 5 bank accounts
- **Complexity:** High (12 steps, parallel tracks, payroll accrual calculations, multiple roles)

## Why This Example Matters

This example shows how the SOP Generator handles:
1. Complex multi-person processes with parallel work streams
2. Calculation-based steps (payroll accrual formula)
3. Strict deadlines and sequences (day 2 cutoff, day 5 draft, day 10 board package)
4. Real-world issues mentioned in the source notes (duplicate payment, post-cutoff invoice disputes)

See SKILL.md for the full source meeting notes and complete generated SOP.

## Key Features Demonstrated

- **Parallel tracks** — Bank reconciliation (David) and AP processing (Jenny) run simultaneously on days 1-2
- **Calculation step** — Payroll accrual includes a worked example with actual numbers
- **Strict cutoff enforcement** — Decision tree at Step 4 handles post-cutoff invoice disputes
- **Timeline overview table** — Maps every task to a business day and owner
- **Real error scenarios** — The $4,200 bank rec discrepancy and post-cutoff invoice disputes came from actual experience
- **Multi-role coordination** — Three people with clearly defined responsibilities and handoff points
