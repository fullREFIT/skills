# LinkedIn Post Text Templates
## Caption Templates for Carousel and Infographic Posts

These templates provide the caption text that accompanies carousel and infographic uploads. The carousel/infographic is the visual; the caption is the context and commentary.

---

## Caption Structure (Carousel Posts)

LinkedIn carousel posts use a different caption strategy than text-only posts. The carousel does the heavy visual lifting; the caption provides context and the "why this matters" framing.

### Recommended Structure

```
[1-2 sentence hook that echoes the carousel's slide 1]

[2-3 sentences of context that the carousel doesn't cover — the "why" behind the "what"]

[1 sentence connecting to the reader's situation]

[CTA line — always "Link in comments" or "Link in first comment"]

#YourBrand
```

### Length

150-250 words maximum. Shorter than a text-only post because the carousel carries the substance. The caption is the frame, not the painting.

---

## Caption Structure (Infographic Posts)

Infographic posts need slightly more caption text because there's only one image.

### Recommended Structure

```
[1-2 sentence hook]

[3-5 sentences expanding on the infographic's insight — add context, story, or implication the image doesn't cover]

[1 sentence bridging to the reader]

[CTA line]

#YourBrand
```

### Length

200-350 words. Closer to a standard LinkedIn post length.

## First Comment Protocol

Immediately after publishing, post the first comment with:

```
[Lead magnet name]: [URL]

Or join the free community for live builds every Thursday: [Skool URL]
```

Keep the first comment short. One or two links maximum. No paragraph of text — the caption already did that work.

---

## Hashtag Rules

- `#YourBrand` on every post (brand tag)
- No other hashtags unless testing specific reach
- No emoji in hashtags
- Hashtag goes at the very end of the caption, separated by a blank line
