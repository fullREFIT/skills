# Content Strategy Guide
## Mapping Pain Points to Carousel and Infographic Content

This reference guides content strategy decisions: which topics become carousels vs. infographics, how to map pain points to narrative arcs, and which lead magnet CTA to assign.

---

## Carousel vs. Infographic Decision

| If the content... | Format | Why |
|-------------------|--------|-----|
| Tells a story with a beginning, middle, and end | **Carousel** (4-6 slides) | Stories need sequence |
| Presents a single stat, comparison, or checklist | **Infographic** (1 image) | One idea, one image |
| Walks through a process or methodology | **Carousel** | Steps need progression |
| Quantifies a single problem or result | **Infographic** | Numbers hit hardest in isolation |
| Demonstrates before/after with context | **Carousel** | Context needs slides 1-2 before the reveal |
| Makes a direct offer with pricing/structure | **Carousel** | The buildup justifies the ask |
| Shares a contrarian take or thesis | **Infographic** (quote card) | Bold statements work as single images |
| Lists evaluation criteria or questions to ask | **Carousel** or **Infographic** | Short list = infographic; annotated list = carousel |

**Primary narrative:** "You can't see inside your sales calls. Here's what you're missing."

| Slide | Content | Template |
|-------|---------|----------|
| 1 | Hook: "47 calls. You know nothing else." | `hook` (big number) |

## CTA Assignment Rules

Every carousel and infographic must end with a specific CTA. Match based on pain point focus:

| Content Targets | Primary CTA | Lead Magnet |
|----------------|-------------|-------------|
| PP2, PP9, PP10, PP11 (sales process) | "Take the Sales Visibility Audit" | Sales Visibility Audit |
| PP1, PP3, PP4, PP8 (key-person risk) | "Run the Calculator" | Key-Person Walkout Calculator |
| PP26, PP28 (capability gap — executive audience) | "Run the Numbers" | The $65K to $120K Calculator |
| PP26, PP27, PP28 (capability gap — operational audience) | "Take the Assessment" | AI Tool Gap Assessment |
| PP6, PP7, PP15, PP16, PP21 (AI readiness) | "Take the AI Readiness Assessment" | AI Readiness Assessment |
| PP26, PP27, PP29 (team audit) | "Score Your Team" | Team AI Capability Audit |
| Content/pipeline topics (PP22-25) | "Join the Community" | Skool community |
| Positioning/offer content | "See It Live" or "Join the Community" | Skool community |

**LinkedIn posting protocol:**
1. Upload the carousel PDF via the document icon (not image)
2. Write the post caption text
3. Publish
4. Immediately post the lead magnet URL as the first comment

Never put the link in the post caption — LinkedIn suppresses posts with external links in the body.

## Post Category Mix

Across a month of LinkedIn carousels and infographics, maintain this distribution:

| Category | % | Purpose |
|----------|---|---------|
| Problem-Awareness | 33% | Name a specific pain the buyer recognizes |
| Story/Proof | 25% | Demonstrate capability with specific work |
| Methodology/Insight | 25% | Share frameworks, contrarian takes |
| Direct Offer | 17% | Explain the service model |

Rotate across symptom clusters. Never post three consecutive pieces targeting the same cluster.
