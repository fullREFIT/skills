# Anti-Patterns — Script Forge Reference

Eight anti-patterns. Any match requires evaluation. Two or more matches in the same piece is a REJECT.

## 1. Positioning Disguise

Claims to teach but actually describes the creator's services. Framework/teaching content is less than 50% of the script. The viewer learns what the creator does, not what they can do.

**Detection:** Remove every sentence about the creator's services. Is there still a complete, actionable teaching framework? If not, it's a positioning disguise.

## 2. Recycled Hook

Same hook pattern with cosmetic text changes across scripts. Two hooks starting with the same 3-word fingerprint within 60 days.

**Detection:** Extract first sentence. Compare against recent inventory.

## 3. Service Description Short

A short video that names a problem and describes what the creator builds to solve it. No standalone insight the viewer can use without hiring anyone.

**Detection:** Does the viewer gain a capability from watching? Or do they just learn that a capability exists and the creator sells it?

## 4. Creator-as-Hero

Script structured around the creator's journey rather than the framework. The creator is subject of more than 20% of sentences in teaching sections. The viewer watches the creator's story instead of learning a method.

**Detection:** Count creator-as-subject sentences vs. reader-as-subject. If the creator exceeds 20%, restructure.

## 5. AI Hype Mirror

Content mirrors AI influencer ecosystem language. Generic AI claims not anchored to operational specifics. Could be said by any AI consultant without modification.

**Detection:** Replace your brand name with any other brand in the same niche. Does the content still work? If yes, it's not specific enough.

## 6. Calendar-Link Nurture

Ends with "book a call" or hard-pitch CTA. Calendar link in email body. Any CTA that sounds like sales rather than a colleague mentioning something useful.

**Detection:** Read the close out loud. Does it sound like a pitch or like mentioning something to a friend?

## 7. Topic Cluster Saturation

The topic, anchor story, or metaphor overlaps with content published or planned within 60 days. Three or more pieces teaching the same core insight.

**Detection:** State the core insight in one sentence. Search the inventory for pieces that teach the same insight. If 3+ exist, this is saturated.

## 8. The Concept Lecture

Long-form content that teaches a mental model but provides no actionable method. The FRAMEWORK explains a concept. The WTODN says "think about" or "ask yourself."

**Detection (Monday Test):** State what the viewer can do Monday they couldn't do Friday. If the answer describes a way of thinking rather than a way of doing, the script fails.

**Fix:** Rewrite the FRAMEWORK around an actionable method. Use the concept as the CONTEXT setup.
